Why is there ethnic disparity in stop and search in County X?

Data

Data on 8,400 stop-and-searches in County X, April 2022 to March 2024

Five potential reasons for disparity

Biased policing officers may be more likely to search people from some ethnic groups due to bias

Biased public public may be more likely to report suspicions about people from some ethnic groups due to bias

Differential offending people from some ethnic groups may be more likely to commit some types of crime

Population structure greater proportion of people from some ethnic groups are young (67% of people searched are under 35)

Differential exposure people from some ethnic groups exposed to policing more often due to patterns in their non-criminal activities.

Conclusion

  • In County X, people of Black, Asian and Mixed ethnicities are more likely to be searched than White people
  • Disparities are not explained by population factors, biases in public reporting or biased policing
  • Disparities may be due to differential offending and/or differential exposure to policing
