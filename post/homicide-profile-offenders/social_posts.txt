Each year about 730 people are charged with a homicide offence in England and Wales. But who are they?

This thread summarises what we know about who commits homicides and in what circumstances.

Most homicide offenders are young, with half of all convicted offenders age between 13 and 26, and a quarter aged between 13 and 19. [132 chrs]

The chart spans ages 10 to 85 and peaks at age 19, where over 70 suspects are recorded. A sharp decline follows into the late twenties, then gradually decreases with age. Very few suspects are aged over 60. Data source is the Home Office Homicide Index, including only cases where the suspect’s age was known.

Most offenders were young, but not for all homicide types. Half of offenders in homicides of male victims under 25 in public places were 19 or younger (three quarters were 22 or younger), but for female victims of domestic homicide half of offenders were 40+. [259 chrs]

Female victims of domestic homicide (16+): Wide age range with a peak in offenders aged 30–40, tapering off gradually on both sides. Median around age 42. Male victims of domestic homicide (16+): Bimodal distribution with prominent peaks around ages 25 and 45. Median around age 33. Male victims under 25 killed in public spaces: Sharp peak around age 19, indicating most offenders were young. Steep drop-off after age 25. Female victims, non-domestic (16+): Flatter distribution with no strong peak, suggesting a wide range of offender ages. Median around 32. Male victims, non-domestic, killed in public spaces: Skewed toward younger offenders with a peak around age 20. Median 25. Other victims: Peak around age 25, with a gradual decline up to age 60, and some offenders older. Median near age 30. Source: Home Office Homicide Index. 93% of convicted homicide offenders were men. 65% of cases involved male victims killed by male suspects, 26% of cases involved female victims killed by male suspects, and 4% of cases involved male victims killed by female suspects. [232 chrs]

----

Most homicide offenders are White (unsurprisingly since 82% of people in England and Wales define themselves as White) but Black offenders are over-represented relative to the population. [187 chrs]

[sus_age_ethnicity.png]

Alt text: Bar chart showing annual homicide suspect rates per million people by age and ethnicity in England and Wales. The chart is divided into five panels: All suspects, Asian, Black, Mixed/Multiple, and White. Bars are color-coded: dark red indicates a lower or equal homicide rate compared to the total population of the same age, while bright red indicates a higher rate. A stepped line in each panel shows the overall homicide offending rate across all ethnicities for reference. Black suspects, especially aged 20–30, show significantly higher homicide suspect rates compared to other groups, with the highest peak exceeding 80 per million. Other groups peak around ages 20–30 but at lower rates. Asian, White, and All suspects show more moderate rates, while Mixed/Multiple suspects have a small peak. The x-axis shows age in 10-year increments; the y-axis shows homicide suspect rate. Data is from the Home Office Homicide Index, covering 95.1% of known-age, known-ethnicity suspects.

----

⚠️ Over-representation does not mean homicide offending is common among a group: even among Black people aged 10–19, only about 30 of the 414,000 people in that group were homicide offenders each year. 99.992% of Black people aged 10–19 were not. [246 chrs]

----

While only 3% of people in England and Wales are unemployed, 60% of homicide offenders were jobless. 35% of all convicted homicide offenders were unemployed White men and a further 13% of all convicted homicide offenders were unemployed Black men. [247 chrs]

----

About a quarter of suspects charged with homicide had a previous conviction for a series offence such as kidnap or wounding with intent, with Black suspects more likely to have a previous serious conviction than White or Asian suspects. [236 chrs]

----

Homicide offending is heavily concentrated among some demographic groups: 27% of all convicted homicide offenders were unemployed White men aged 20–49, despite that demographic making up only 0.9% of the population. [215 chrs]

----

Most homicide victims were killed by someone they knew: 40% of charged suspects were acquaintances of the victim, 17% were a family member and 17% were a partner/ex-partner. Only 26% of suspects were strangers. [210 chrs]

----

Of all female victims of homicide, 48% were killed by a current or former partner, compared to only 4% of male victims.

Conversely, 32% of male victims were killed by strangers, compared to 12% of female victims. [213 chrs]

Victims were also more likely to be killed by someone of the same ethnicity as them. This wasn't driven solely by suspects and victims knowing one another: victims killed by strangers were also more likely to be killed by someone of the same ethnicity. [252 chrs]
