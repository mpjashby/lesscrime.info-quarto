Each week, about 12 people are killed in homicides in England and Wales. But who is most likely to be killed?

This thread summarises what we know about who is most likely to be a victim of homicide.

🧵 [202 chrs]

Men (especially young men) are much more likely to be victims of homicide than women are: about 73% of homicide victims are male. [129 chrs]

----

For men, homicide risk peaks at 15–24 then drops. For women, risk is largely constant through adulthood.

Young children are at high risk: girls are more likely to be killed before their 1st birthday than at any other time in their lives. [238 chrs]

Two thirds of homicide victims in England and Wales are White, 15% are Black, 8% are Asian and 9% are of other or unknown ethnicities. [134 chrs]

----

Black people in England and Wales have a higher homicide victimisation rate than the population as a whole at every age, as do Asian adolescents. People from mixed/multiple ethnic backgrounds are less likely to be homicide victims. [231 chrs]

Black men and boys are particularly likely to be victims of homicide: a Black boy aged 10–17 is 13.4 times as likely to be a victim of homicide as a White boy of the same age. [175 chrs]

Black people are at a higher risk of being killed for all types of homicide except non-domestic homicides of women. Asian men are also at higher risk than White men of being killed in domestic homicides. [203 chrs]

[vic_cluster.png]

Each chart includes five ethnic groups: Black, Other, Asian, White, and Mixed/Multiple. Dashed lines show the average for each sub-type. Black individuals consistently show higher victimisation rates, especially among male victims under 25 killed in public (peaking near 60 per million). Data is from the Home Office Homicide Index, excluding the Grays lorry deaths.

----

Unemployed people are 16 times more likely to be victims of homicide than the population as a whole. Unemployed men aged 30–49 make up 0.9% of the population but 16% of all homicide victims. [190 chrs]

----

So while homicide is a rare event for every group in society, it's much more common for some groups than others. For example, all else being equal a Black male aged 16–24 is more than 100 times more likely to be a victim than an Asian male aged under 16. [254 chrs]

Most homicides occur during non-domestic fights (mostly between acquaintances, sometimes strangers) or domestic abuse (mainly between partners, sometimes family members).

All homicides are tragedies, but most happen in tragically everyday circumstances. [254 chrs]

----

Other types of homicide are much rarer: only 1% of homicides had a sexual motive and only 0.2% occurred during terrorist attacks, even though those types of homicide often dominate public debates about public safety. [216 chrs]
